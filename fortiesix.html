Producer & Artist Roster · Spring 2026 · Stockholm / UK

FortieSix

Stockholm producer collective shaping the next generation of rap, pop, alt-indie and afrobeats.

01 / Producers

  • Henry

    Pop · R&B · Afrobeats

    Producer working at the intersection of rap and contemporary pop. Recent credits include Yasin, Lekaye and executive production for Malik, including a Top 4 single in Sweden. Known for pairing strong artist development with commercially minded production.

    • TikTok

  • Rivelino

    Funk · Hip-Hop · Rap

    Producer, engineer and multi-instrumentalist whose technical expertise forms the backbone of FortieSix. Drawing on Brazilian musical influences and a deep understanding of record-making, he moves seamlessly between rap, pop and afrobeats. Recent credits include Cortex and Naliyah.

    • Instagram

  • Baaba

    R&B · House

    Multi-instrumentalist, producer and international DJ based in Stockholm. Working across R&B, afrobeats and contemporary rap, while building his house music project MAMADOU. Recent appearances include Cultur FM, Outlander Magazine and performances across Ibiza — bringing together West African influences, Scandinavian precision and global club culture.
